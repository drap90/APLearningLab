What we make

AP Learning Lab LLC creates digital guides for neurodivergent families, starting with parents of autistic children. Each guide covers one stage of the journey, from first concerns to diagnosis, insurance, benefits and school.

Every guide is written in plain language and designed to be printed. You'll find scripts you can read word for word, checklists, and worksheets to bring to appointments.

Our promise

We explain your options clearly and fairly, and point you to official sources so you can confirm what applies to your family. Rules and programs change, so each guide notes when its information was checked.
